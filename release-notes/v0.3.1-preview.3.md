# Atraium 0.3.1 Preview 3

Atraium 0.3.1 Preview 3 is a compatibility update for [Preview 2](https://github.com/daemosofchaos/Atraium-Preview/releases/tag/v0.3.1-preview.2). It is recommended for users of compatible open-weight reasoning models.

## What changed

- Improved thinking support for compatible Qwen-family and other open-weight reasoning models connected through Ollama.
- Reasoning produced separately from the final answer is now retained and shown in Atraium's reasoning display.
- Final answers remain clean and separate from the model's thinking output.
- Improved compatibility with OpenAI-compatible services that provide reasoning separately from the final response.
- Clearer feedback when a tool cannot complete an action, helping compatible assistants adjust and recover.

To enable thinking on a compatible Ollama model, configure **Reasoning effort** as **Low**, **Medium**, or **High** in its Atraium model profile. You do not need to add `/think` to individual prompts.

## Platforms

- **Windows desktop:** recommended preview experience.
- **Android:** early sideloaded preview for Android 7.0 or later, supporting arm64-v8a devices and x86_64 emulators.
- **Web, macOS, and iOS:** not included in this preview.

## Important notes

- This is preview software and may contain breaking issues.
- Thinking and reasoning support depends on the selected model and the capabilities exposed by its provider or local model server.
- Some compatible models may not support every reasoning-effort level.
- Provider features, model behaviour, quotas, regions, and pricing vary.
- Support is best-effort.

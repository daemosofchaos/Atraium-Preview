# Atraium 0.6.0 Preview 1

Keep your best working methods ready for the next project. Atraium 0.6.0 Preview 1 introduces **Skills**, makes it easier to ask questions with the right guidance, and brings clearer controls to coordinated assistant work.

## Features and improvements

### Your best workflows, ready to use again

Introducing **Skills**: reusable guidance for the way you want work done. Keep your writing style, research approach, coding standards and review checklists ready—without repeating the same instructions in every conversation.

- **Create or import a Skill.** Write your own instructions, add useful checklists and templates, or bring in a Skill from a trusted source.
- **Use it where it belongs.** Keep a personal library, share guidance across a Room, or tailor it to one workspace.
- **Choose when it applies.** Let the assistant choose relevant Skills, ask for one when you need it, or turn it off for a particular project.
- **Save a workflow worth repeating.** Choose **Save this workflow as a Skill** on a completed response, refine the draft and make it available when you're happy with it.
- **Stay in control of updates.** Improve a Skill over time while existing projects keep their chosen version until you update them.
- **Give specialists a consistent approach.** Share the Skills each member of a Supervisor team needs for its assignment.

[Get started with Skills](../docs/skills.md).

### A clearer place to ask

Choose a Skill from the message box without rewriting your question. Requested Skills appear as removable chips, while **Ask about this** keeps selected conversation text in its own card. Review or remove either before sending.

Drafts stay with their workspace as you navigate, and **Reuse last prompt** brings back your question together with its requested Skills and selections.

### Smoother team coordination

- **Keep progress out of the way.** Open Supervisor assignments when you need it, with separate **Active** and **History** views.
- **Tidy history without losing work.** Clear finished assignments from the view and show them again later; conversations and results are retained.
- **Stop a team from the message box.** Stop the current assignment and its team, then start fresh once it has finished closing.
- **Finish the handover.** The Supervisor can still review results and prepare the final summary when specialists reach their limits.
- **Only see the panel when you need it.** Turning off Supervisor removes the assignments panel without deleting its history.

[Supervisor guide](../docs/supervisor-workspaces.md).

### Also improved

- More consistent settings pages, with better use of space on small screens and with enlarged text.
- More reliable PDF creation on Windows.

## Platforms

- **Windows x64:** recommended preview experience, with installer and portable options.
- **Android:** early sideloaded preview for Android 7.0 or later, supporting arm64-v8a devices and x86_64 emulators. Local model servers must be reachable from the device; heavy image and video generation runs on a connected service or server, not on the phone.
- **Web, macOS and iOS:** not included in this preview distribution.

## Important notes

- **Back up important data before upgrading.** Preview versions may change settings or data formats. Backup restore replaces the data covered by the archive rather than merging it; review the confirmation and expect to re-enter API keys.
- **Supervisor delegation is opt-in.** Set team limits and give specialists separate responsibilities. Work can continue while you navigate within the running app, but closing Atraium can interrupt it. Unfinished assignments do not restart automatically.
- **Stopping does not undo completed actions.** Check saved results and external changes before retrying. Wait for work to finish closing before reusing a workspace, changing its settings or retrying a blocked deletion.
- **File tools have full access by default within your operating-system permissions.** Room and workspace settings can require directory access requests. On Windows, these requests take effect immediately rather than opening another approval dialog. These controls do not grant administrator privileges or sandbox commands, plugins or other software. See [Tools and access](../docs/workspaces-and-tools.md).
- **Review Skills before use or sharing.** Import from trusted sources and remove confidential project details from workflow drafts. Skills do not change tool or file access.

## Known limitations

This is preview software, not a guarantee of production readiness or unattended safety.

- Model and tool support varies by provider. A successful connection does not guarantee reliable work with every tool or large request.
- Skills support written instructions and text references; bundled scripts are not run. Important results still need your review.
- Windows and Android do not offer identical tool coverage. Mobile suspension can interrupt background work and downloads.
- Large documents, media and slower storage can still take time to prepare.

See the [full preview scope](../PREVIEW.md) and [Troubleshooting](../docs/troubleshooting.md).

## Downloads

Choose the Windows installer for a standard installation, the portable ZIP to run without installing, or the APK to sideload on Android. The Android preview ZIP includes the APK and supporting guides.

Verify each download against the accompanying **SHA-256 checksum manifest** before installing or running it. If the checksum differs, do not use the file. Follow [Getting started](../GETTING-STARTED.md) for installation and model setup, using the filenames and checksums for the release you downloaded.

## Costs and credentials

**Atraium is a bring-your-own-model application. It does not include provider credentials, model credits, a token allowance or hosting. You are responsible for charges incurred through your connected accounts and services.**

- Chat, Skills, research, OCR, images, video, speech and paid integrations can create charges. Consumer chat subscriptions do not necessarily include API credits.
- Teams and longer projects may make many requests. Set **provider-side budgets, quotas and hard spending limits where available** before starting. Team request limits, spending alerts and Atraium's usage display are not hard spending controls.
- Local models can avoid hosted per-token charges, but hardware, electricity, storage and rented servers still use resources. A local Chat model does not make other hosted capabilities free.
- Keep API keys private. Cancellation may not prevent charges for requests already accepted by a provider.

**Provider-side billing records are authoritative.** Read [Token usage and provider billing](../TOKEN-USAGE-AND-BILLING.md) before connecting paid services.

# Atraium 0.4 Preview 1

Atraium 0.4 Preview 1 lets multiple assistants work at the same time while you move between projects. A new activity menu keeps their progress and controls within reach, and Rooms now remember where you left off. It follows [0.3.4 Preview 9](https://github.com/daemosofchaos/Atraium-Preview/releases/tag/v0.3.4-preview.9).

## What changed

### Keep work moving across projects

- Text assistants now continue working when you switch Rooms or Workspaces. Start research in one Workspace and move to another without interrupting it.
- Returning to a Workspace brings back its conversation, streamed progress, unsent draft, and selected attachments. Each Workspace has its own independent Stop control.

### See every busy assistant

- Open **Assistant activity** beside the theme switcher to see the live busy count, current steps, elapsed time, short request summaries, and recent outcomes across Rooms and Workspaces.
- Use **Open workspace** to jump directly to an assistant or **Stop** to cancel only its current work. The **Here** marker identifies your current Workspace, with keyboard-friendly controls and layouts that adapt to smaller screens.

### Pick up where you left off

- Each Room remembers its last selected Workspace, including after restarting Atraium. Search and activity links still open their intended destination; deleted or archived selections fall back safely.

### More reliable control and background updates

- Fixed Stop becoming unavailable or ineffective after automatic context continuation or Computer Use Handoff. It now remains connected through those transitions, while keeping any partial response text.
- Computer Use Handoffs take turns controlling the desktop. Waiting assistants show their status, and stopping a waiting handoff does not interrupt the assistant already using the device.
- Improved handling of simultaneous updates so background activity does not unexpectedly switch your Room or overwrite unrelated task edits.

## Platforms

- **Windows desktop:** recommended preview experience.
- **Android:** early sideloaded preview for Android 7.0 or later, supporting arm64-v8a devices and x86_64 emulators.
- **Web, macOS, and iOS:** not included in this preview.

## Important notes

- This is preview software; support is best-effort.
- Parallel background work covers text chats, not simultaneous voice sessions. Keep Atraium open; running work does not automatically resume after an app restart.
- Assistants cannot yet delegate work to other Rooms or Workspaces.
- Shared desktop access does not prevent conflicts when assistants edit the same files. Stop does not undo completed actions and may take longer when an external service is slow to cancel.

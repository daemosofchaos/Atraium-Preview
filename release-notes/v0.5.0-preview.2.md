# Atraium 0.5.0 Preview 2

Atraium 0.5.0 Preview 2 is a maintenance update that improves Room editing and the in-app guidance for Room and workspace settings.

## Fixes and improvements

- **More reliable Room editing.** Improved opening and saving of Room settings.
- **Clearer settings guidance.** Corrected guide positioning and improved settings layouts on smaller screens and with enlarged text.

## Platforms

- **Windows desktop:** recommended preview experience.
- **Android:** early sideloaded preview for Android 7.0 or later. Local model servers must be reachable from the device; heavy image and video inference runs on a hosted service or remote server, not on the phone.
- **Web, macOS and iOS:** not included in this preview distribution.

## Important notes

- This is preview software. **Back up important data before upgrading.** Provider availability and capabilities vary.
- **Supervisor delegation is opt-in.** Team limits are shared across nested workers; request limits are not a currency spending cap. Work can continue while you navigate within the running app, but not after Atraium closes. Interrupted assignments are not automatically restarted.
- **Stopping work does not undo completed actions.** Review saved results and external changes before retrying. Room or workspace deletion remains unavailable while cleanup is pending.
- **File tools have full access by default within your operating-system permissions.** Room and workspace settings can require directory access requests. On Windows, these requests grant access immediately rather than opening a separate approval dialog. File-tool controls do not grant administrator privileges or sandbox terminal commands, plugins or other software.
- **Guides do not send sample conversations, run connection tests or download models automatically.** Replaying a guide does not reset saved connections or work.
- **Backup restore replaces the data covered by the archive rather than merging it.** Review the confirmation and re-enter API keys after restoring.
- Mobile suspension can pause background work, tracking and downloads. Playback and local model access depend on the device and services you configure.

## Costs and credentials

**Atraium is a bring-your-own-model application.** It does not include provider credentials, model credits, a token allowance or hosting. You are responsible for charges incurred through the accounts and services you configure.

- Chat, research, OCR, images, video, speech and paid integrations can incur separate charges. A consumer ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini subscription does not necessarily include API credits. A local Chat model does not make other hosted capabilities free.
- Supervisor teams, longer projects and follow-up work can involve many model and tool calls. Set **provider-side budgets, quotas and hard spending limits where available** before starting. Alerts and Atraium's usage display are not hard spending controls.
- Local models can avoid hosted per-token charges, but hardware, electricity, storage and rented servers can still cost money. Hosted services receive the prompts and selected content needed for their features.
- Keep API keys private. Do not include credentials in prompts, screenshots or public bug reports. Cancellation may not prevent charges for requests already accepted by a provider.

**Provider-side billing records are authoritative.** Review [Token usage and provider billing](https://github.com/daemosofchaos/Atraium-Preview/blob/main/TOKEN-USAGE-AND-BILLING.md) before connecting paid services.

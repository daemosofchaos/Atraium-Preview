# Atraium 0.4.3 Preview 1

Atraium 0.4.3 Preview 1 adds video generation, supports GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst and Flare, and improves how generated media is tracked, played and saved.

## What changed

### Create videos from a prompt or image

- Generate short clips with hosted **fal** presets: **Kling 3.0 Standard, Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0** (provider preview), and **LTX 2.5 Fast**. Connect a local or remote **ComfyUI** server for **Wan 2.2 5B** or **LTX 2.5**.
- Configure resolution, aspect ratio, audio and a maximum clip length in **Model providers**. Built-in video presets remove the need to supply workflow JSON. **Test connection** checks the connection and required setup without generating a clip; unsupported options are rejected before submission.
- Job cards show preparation, queueing, generation and download status. Accepted jobs can reconnect after a restart without automatically submitting another generation. Cancellation feedback distinguishes a cancellation request from a confirmed cancellation.
- Load completed clips on demand, play them with controls and no autoplay, or save the original MP4/WebM file. Larger videos can be exported individually up to **200 MiB**, without regenerating or converting them.

### Keep video tools focused

- Video actions use **Gateway** by default. The new **Video Generation** Workspace template makes those actions **Direct** in that Workspace only, while preserving Room settings and explicit tool choices. The template does not configure a provider or grant additional permissions.

### GPT Image 2.5 generation and editing

- Added **Sunburst** and **Flare** model choices, including their dated snapshots. Both support generation and edits, with additional quality levels, valid custom dimensions, transparent PNG/WebP output and JPEG/WebP compression.
- Improved OpenAI-compatible and Azure image connections. Edits retain the requested background and compression settings, and reference images keep their original format. Existing model profiles and custom deployment names are not automatically changed.

### More reliable saved media

- Videos that finish after the text response remain attached to the correct conversation and survive subsequent background saves. Loading conversation history no longer loads every generated video's contents before playback is requested.

## Platforms

- **Windows desktop:** recommended preview experience.
- **Android:** early sideloaded preview for Android 7.0 or later. Video inference runs through a hosted service or remote GPU server, not on the phone.
- **Web, macOS and iOS:** not included in this preview.

## Important notes

- **Stopping chat or navigating away does not cancel video generation.** Use **Cancel video** for an explicit cancellation request. Hosted cancellation is best-effort and may still be billed; running ComfyUI jobs are not interrupted server-wide.
- Mobile suspension can pause tracking and downloads. Reopen the original message to reconnect. Playback depends on device memory and codec support.
- Audio and image-reference support vary by preset. ComfyUI requires installed models and compatible nodes; model licensing terms apply. Hosted generation sends prompts and selected references to the provider.
- This is preview software; provider access, availability and capabilities vary.

## Costs and credentials

Atraium does not include provider credentials, model credits or hosting. Image and video generation can incur separate charges. Set provider-side spending limits and review [Token usage and provider billing](https://github.com/daemosofchaos/Atraium-Preview/blob/main/TOKEN-USAGE-AND-BILLING.md).

Website redesign

A focused refresh for a small consulting firm.

Purpose

Make the firm's services easier to understand and help prospective clients send an enquiry. Delivery is planned over four weeks with one developer.

Decision required: Approve the page scope and nominate one person to provide content and review each milestone.

Deliverables

DeliverableAcceptance
HomepageClear positioning, services overview and enquiry action.
Services pagesApproved content and consistent navigation on desktop and mobile.
Contact formValidated fields, accessible errors and confirmed delivery route.
HandoverDeployment instructions, ownership details and maintenance checklist.

Delivery plan

  1. Week 1: Agree content, page structure and visual direction.
  2. Week 2: Implement the core pages and shared layout.
  3. Week 3: Add the form and complete device/accessibility checks.
  4. Week 4: Review, resolve issues and prepare the handover.

Assumptions and next steps

Client content and brand assets are available before implementation. No customer portal, commerce or migration is included. Pricing and launch dates require confirmation.

Confirm scope, provide content and choose the review owner before work starts.
